# Glossary

- **Harness**: repeatable engineering workflow built from a controller, role agents, rules, skills, Wiki, and gates.
- **Main Agent**: coordinator that decomposes work, routes stages, checks gates, handles rollback, and summarizes delivery.
- **Requirements baseline**: clarified scope, scenarios, criteria, and risks used for design and acceptance.
- **Stage gate**: minimum quality conditions required before entering the next stage.
- **Traceable evidence**: diff, command output, test result, screenshot, log, or explicit unverified record.
- **Conditional pass**: core delivery is usable with recorded low/medium risks and follow-up ownership.
